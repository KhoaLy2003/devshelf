## BLOCKED and observations

- S18 (AC-24): no way to run the app on a modified copy, and editing live rows was out of scope. Not run.
- S04/S06: all AC-03 messages and the exact 4096-byte boundary (ASCII and multibyte) were run with real input in launch 3. The empty-value, long-label and over-limit checks are also enforced by Rust (direct IPC probe, error codes in the rows above); those probes bypass the webview and are not real UI input.
- S16: the secrets list is cached by react-query across route changes; an external change to the DB (or a project removed elsewhere) shows up after a reload or after a refetch, not on navigating back to Secrets.
- S10: pointer movement on a row resets both the reveal idle and the vault timeout (six moves, six resets to 1:00); the 30 s and 60 s gaps were measured from the last dispatched move. Whether the six moves hit the revealed row: the move script took its coordinates from the bounding box of the first secret row (x at 30% of the row width, y at its vertical centre, alternating +0/+25 px), and that first row was the revealed one (id 3, "E2E AL"); the individual coordinates were not logged. The behaviour itself shows they were on that row: the row handlers (`onPointerMove` on the row `li`, `src/screens/secrets-screen.tsx`) are the only thing that resets a row's 30 s idle, and the row stayed revealed for about 86 s after the Reveal click (still revealed at M + 26.1 s with M = Reveal + 60.01 s), which off-row moves could not have done; it then re-masked at M + 30.012 s. This is inference from behaviour, not a logged bounding-box check.
- Console: one "Failed to load resource: ... 404" per launch (source not identified; request hosts were only Vite and IPC); a Chromium DOM hint about password forms without a username field.
- Timings: row re-mask 30.005 s (idle), 50.962 s (after a move at 20.04 s); with the 1-minute timeout, re-mask at 30.012 s and lock at 60.758 s after the last move.
